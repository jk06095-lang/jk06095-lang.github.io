ECHO Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-03

This English text is a translation of the Korean original, provided for your convenience. If the two differ, the Korean text governs.

The short version

Echo recognizes your conversations and keeps them as text so you can learn from them. Recordings are not stored on the server. The data used to recognize your voice stays on this device only, and you can delete it at any time.

⚠ Legal notice about recording conversations

Live Talk recognizes the speech of people around you. The Republic of Korea's Protection of Communications Secrets Act prohibits secretly recording or listening in on a conversation you are not a party to. Before you turn this feature on, you must tell the other person and get their consent. Any legal liability arising from using it without consent is yours.

Echo does not save the other person's voice as a file. Only the recognized text remains. During recognition, however, the audio is sent to the companies in the table below.

Processors and transfers abroad

The companies below process data on Echo's behalf, and Echo gives personal data to no one else. Speech recognition and AI responses are processed on the servers of companies outside Korea — sent in real time over an encrypted connection the moment you use a conversation or practice feature. After processing, each company keeps or deletes the data under its own policy, and Echo neither views nor controls what is stored on those servers. If you do not agree to the transfer abroad, you can decline on the first-run consent screen; Echo's speech recognition and AI conversation are then unavailable.

There are two routes. Audio goes from the glasses to the recognition company directly. Text requests (translation · expression suggestions · building situations · speech synthesis) go through Echo's server — so that the keys are not kept in the app. It only passes through; the content is not stored. How we protect it. All transfers are encrypted with TLS. Server records are protected by per-account access control (row-level security) so that only your account can read them, and vendor API keys are kept on the server, never in the app. Original recordings and your voiceprint never leave your device.

How long we keep it

We keep your learning records and account information (email · social links) until you delete them in the app or ask us to delete them at the contact below. Because the point of learning is comparing against past records, we do not set a period and delete them automatically. You can delete them whenever you want in the ways below; requests by email are handled within 10 days.

Open source we use

We use the WeSpeaker model (onnx-community/wespeaker-voxceleb-resnet34-LM) to tell voices apart — CC BY 4.0. We use ONNX Runtime Web (MIT) and Transformers.js (Apache-2.0) for audio processing.
